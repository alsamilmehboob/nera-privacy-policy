NERA Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

NERA ("NERA", "we", "us", or "our") is a traditional board game developed and published as a mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how NERA handles information when you use the NERA mobile application, including its online multiplayer features.

2. Information We Collect

NERA is designed to minimize the amount of personal information required to play the game.

Anonymous Authentication

When you use online multiplayer features, NERA uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create an anonymous identifier for your game session.

Anonymous authentication does not require you to provide your real name, email address, phone number, or password.

Player Name or Nickname

NERA may allow you to enter a player name or nickname when using multiplayer features.

Providing a player name or nickname is optional.

If you provide a player name or nickname, it may be shared with the other player in the same online match.

You should not enter sensitive personal information into a player name or nickname.

Online Game Information

When using online multiplayer, NERA transmits information required to operate and synchronize the match. This may include the game room code, anonymous player identifier, player name or nickname when provided, game moves, placements, scores, round information, game status, and message timestamps.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by NERA is used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating online multiplayer games.
  • Creating and managing anonymous game sessions.
  • Connecting players through online game rooms.
  • Synchronizing game state between players.
  • Displaying player names or nicknames during multiplayer games.
  • Maintaining the reliability and security of the game.
  • Detecting and resolving technical problems.
  • Preventing misuse of the online multiplayer service.

4. Firebase and Cloud Firestore

NERA uses services provided by Google Firebase to support its online multiplayer functionality.

NERA uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create an anonymous identifier for online game sessions.

NERA also uses Google Cloud Firestore to support online multiplayer communication. Game messages may contain the anonymous player identifier, message type, game information, and a server-generated timestamp.

Firebase and Google may process information according to their applicable privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

NERA does not sell your personal information.

When you use online multiplayer, information necessary to operate the match is processed through Firebase and Cloud Firestore.

A player name or nickname that you choose to provide may be shared with the other player in the same online match.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of NERA and its users.

6. Data Retention

Online multiplayer information is retained only as necessary for the operation, security, maintenance, and troubleshooting of the online game service, subject to the configuration and operation of the underlying Firebase services.

NERA does not require users to maintain a traditional personal account containing information such as an email address, phone number, or password.

7. Data and Deletion Requests

NERA uses anonymous authentication for online multiplayer and does not require users to create a traditional account using an email address or password.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information associated with your NERA game session, you may contact us using the email address provided below.

We will review reasonable privacy and data-related requests and take appropriate action where applicable.

8. Children's Privacy

NERA is not specifically directed toward children and does not intentionally request children to provide personal information such as their real name, email address, phone number, or home address.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through NERA, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through NERA and its supporting services.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to NERA, its features, services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

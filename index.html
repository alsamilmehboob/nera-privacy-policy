NERA Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

NERA ("NERA", "we", "us", or "our") is a traditional board game developed and published as a mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how NERA handles information when you use the NERA mobile application, including its online multiplayer features.

By using NERA, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

NERA is designed to minimize the amount of personal information required to play the game.

Anonymous authentication

When you use online multiplayer features, NERA may use Firebase Anonymous Authentication to create an anonymous account identifier for your game session.

This anonymous authentication does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or password.

Player name or nickname

NERA may allow you to enter a player name or nickname when using multiplayer features.

Providing a name or nickname is optional unless a particular game feature specifically states otherwise.

You should not enter sensitive personal information into a player name or nickname.

Game information

When using online multiplayer, information required to operate the game may be transmitted between players and our backend services. This may include game-room information, game state, player identifiers, player names or nicknames, and information necessary to synchronize an online match.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by NERA may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate online multiplayer games.
  • To create and manage anonymous game sessions.
  • To synchronize game state between players.
  • To display player names or nicknames during multiplayer games.
  • To maintain and improve the reliability and security of the game.
  • To diagnose technical problems and prevent abuse of the service.

4. Firebase and Third-Party Services

NERA may use third-party services provided by Google Firebase to support functionality such as anonymous authentication, online multiplayer, database services, analytics, crash reporting, or other technical services that may be enabled in future versions.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

NERA does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide the game's functionality, such as Firebase services used for authentication, online multiplayer, database functionality, security, diagnostics, or other technical services.

We may also disclose information when required to do so by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of NERA and its users.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant game functionality, maintain security, resolve technical problems, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Game-room and multiplayer information may be temporary and may be removed when it is no longer required for the operation of the service.

7. Account and Data Deletion

NERA uses anonymous authentication for online multiplayer and does not require users to create a traditional account using an email address or password.

If NERA later introduces account-based features or persistent user profiles, appropriate account and data deletion functionality will be provided in accordance with applicable requirements.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information associated with your NERA game session, you may contact us using the contact information provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

NERA is a general-audience game and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information such as their real name, email address, phone number, or home address.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through NERA and its supporting services.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to NERA, its features, services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
